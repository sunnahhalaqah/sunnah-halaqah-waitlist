02 · How it works

One halaqah, two ways in.

For learners

  1. 1Tell us what you want to learn and where you are now.
  2. 2We help you find a teacher who fits your level and goals.
  3. 3Study one-to-one or in a group, at a pace that works for you.

For teachers

  1. 1Tell us what you teach and your experience.
  2. 2We introduce you to learners who need your skill.
  3. 3Teach what you know, and earn through it.
